# Academy Outreach — Email Sequence & DM Scripts

Personalise every send: the `[...]` slots are mandatory, not optional. One specific sentence about *their* academy (a course they run, a student post you saw) is what separates a reply from a delete. Send from a real person's address (e.g. rene@gelitup.com), not a noreply.

All links UTM-tagged — see playbook §6.

English works for NL, GR (or Greek), Scandinavia, PL (younger owners). For IT, ES, FR expect meaningfully higher reply rates in the local language — translate Email 1 before those batches. The site's i18n already supports multiple languages; match the landing page language to the email language where available.
