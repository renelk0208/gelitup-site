# EU Nail Academy Outreach Campaign — Playbook

Goal: get independent EU nail academies to register a B2B account and place a first (sample/starter-kit) order via **gelitup.com/for-academies**.

**Excluded markets: Greece and Bulgaria are NOT targeted** — no outreach, no localised content, exclude both in any paid geo targeting.

Everything the campaign promises must match what the site already claims: no minimum order for registered academies, free EU shipping (zones 2–5), HEMA-free + TPO-free formulas, CPNP-notified / EC 1223/2009 compliant, Leaping Bunny approved, SDS documentation on request, Academy Starter Kit, sample availability before committing, WhatsApp/Viber support.

---

## 1. The hook: TPO compliance

The EU banned **TPO** (trimethylbenzoyl diphenylphosphine oxide, a common gel photoinitiator) in cosmetic products from **1 September 2025**. As of 2026, academies teaching with old TPO-containing stock are training students on products that are illegal to supply in the EU — and academies are *exactly* the audience that cares, because they teach compliance.

This is the strongest opener for cold outreach: it's timely, it's a real business risk for them, and GEL.IT.UP is TPO-free with the paperwork to prove it. HEMA-free is the second hook — student allergy risk during training (over-exposure to uncured product is highest among learners).

## 2. Segments (see eu-academy-leads.csv)

- **High priority — independent academies** with in-person, consumable-heavy programmes (18-day / 80h / 160h courses burn a lot of product). These buy repeatedly at every intake cycle.
- **Medium — online academies and influencer educators.** Lower product volume themselves, but they recommend kits to hundreds of students. Treat as partnership/affiliate targets, not just customers.
- **Chains (École Terrade, Oligenesi, Schoonheidsschool)** — slower to close, but one yes = many campuses. Personal, patient outreach.
- **Skip — brand-owned academies** (Semilac, ProNails, Alessandro, Moyra, emmi-NAIL, OA Nail System). They exist to sell their own product line.

Nail Tales Academy (Greece) is already a GEL.IT.UP partner — Greece isn't a target market, but they still work as social proof in outreach ("we already supply academies like Nail Tales Academy").

## 3. The offer ladder

1. **Free/cheap first taste:** sample shades + SDS pack sent before any commitment (the site already promises "sample availability before you commit").
2. **Academy Starter Kit** at academy wholesale rates — positioned as "run one class on us/at cost, judge the product on your students' results."
3. **Intake-cycle supply:** wholesale pricing, no MOQ, free EU shipping, restock timed to their course calendar — most academies run September and January intakes, so July/August and November/December are the buying windows.
4. **Partnership tier:** co-branded student kits, listing on the site as a training partner, referral/affiliate margin on student kit purchases after graduation. Students who learn on GEL.IT.UP buy GEL.IT.UP for their whole career — say this out loud in the pitch; it's the academy's lever to add value to *their* offer too ("your students get a professional trade account on day one").

## 4. Channels, in order of effort/return

1. **Email** (templates in `outreach-emails.md`) — to the academy's published business address.
2. **Instagram DM** — academies live on Instagram; DMs from the GEL.IT.UP brand account often outperform email in this industry. Comment on their student-work posts first, DM second.
3. **WhatsApp** — only after they respond somewhere (cold WhatsApp is intrusive + risky under ePrivacy).
4. **Retargeting/lookalike ads** later, once the page gets UTM-tagged traffic.

## 5. GDPR / ePrivacy — read before sending

- Emailing a **generic company address** (info@academy.com) about a relevant B2B matter is generally defensible as legitimate interest in most member states. Emailing a **named person** without consent is riskier.
- **Germany and Austria are strict**: even B2B cold email effectively requires consent (UWG §7). For DE/AT leads, use their website **contact forms** or Instagram instead of cold email.
- Every email must: identify GEL.IT.UP/GIUP with full company details, be honest in the subject line, and include a working opt-out. Log opt-outs and never re-contact.
- Keep volume low and personal (10–20/day, manually personalised). No bought lists, no scraping personal data.

## 7. Cadence

- **Week 1–2:** Netherlands + Poland (email-friendly, high priority rows).
- **Week 2–3:** Italy, Spain, France, Belgium.
- **Ongoing:** Germany + Austria via contact forms and Instagram only.
- Per lead: Email 1 → wait 5–7 days → Email 2 → wait 7 days → Email 3 (break-up) → move to quarterly newsletter only if they never opted out and replied at least once; otherwise stop.

## 8. KPI targets (first 90 days)

- 50+ academies contacted, ≥30% reply rate (this is niche B2B with a real hook — generic blasts get 2%, personalised should get far more)
- 10+ sample packs / starter kits out the door
- 5+ registered academy accounts, 3+ first orders
- 1 named training partner willing to be referenced (beyond Nail Tales)
