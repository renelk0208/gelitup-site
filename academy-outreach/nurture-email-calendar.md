# Bi-Weekly Academy Email — Rotating Theme Calendar

Audience: opted-in contacts only (sample-kit claimants, registered academies, newsletter signups).
Platform: Zoho Campaigns. Send every second Tuesday, 12:00–14:00 CET.
Every email: one theme, one CTA, under 300 words. Footer always has unsubscribe + company details.

## The six-theme wheel (repeat after email 6)

Each theme returns only every ~12 weeks, so the list never sees the same "shape" twice in a row.

### 1. Compliance Corner
What changed / what to check in EU cosmetics rules for nail products. TPO ban aftermath, HEMA sensitisation stats, SDS housekeeping, label checks.
**CTA:** "Need the paperwork for our products? Reply and it's yours." (replies = engagement = deliverability gold)
**Why it works:** academies *teach* the standard — this is the content only a serious supplier sends.

### 2. Technique / Classroom Tip
One teachable trick instructors can use in the next class — builder structure, cure windows, prep for problem nails. Written educator-to-educator, product mentions only where natural.
**CTA:** link to a relevant catalogue category or Inspiration page.

### 3. Product Spotlight
One product or system, told through the classroom lens: why it behaves well in training volume, batch consistency, what students find easy/hard about it.
**CTA:** wholesale price list / portal login.

### 4. Academy Spotlight / Social Proof
A partner academy's story, student results, or a Q&A with an instructor. Start with Nail Tales Academy; every new partner becomes future content. (Always get written permission — and being featured is itself a perk you can offer partners.)
**CTA:** "Want your academy featured? Reply."

### 5. Season / Intake Planner
Timed to the academic calendar: July & Nov–Dec = "restock before intake" (Sept/Jan starts), spring = trend shades for student portfolios, summer = plan next year's kits.
**CTA:** /academy-kit for new contacts' colleagues, or portal order link for registered ones.

### 6. The Offer / Nudge
The only overtly commercial one per cycle: starter-kit pricing, a bundle, free-shipping reminder, "first 50 kits" style urgency.
**CTA:** order / register.

## Keeping it fresh mechanically (Zoho Campaigns features)

- **A/B test every subject line** (Campaigns picks the winner automatically after a few hours). Alternate subject styles: question ("Is your autumn stock TPO-clean?"), number ("3 things students break in week one"), plain ("September intake checklist").
- **Merge tags:** first name + academy name in body copy, never in the subject (spam signal).
- **Dynamic content blocks:** one email, different paragraphs by segment —
  - by **students-per-season** (the form captures it): 100+ academies see bulk/pallet framing; 1–10 see starter framing.
  - by **status:** kit-claimed-but-never-ordered gets a gentle nudge block; ordered customers get reorder blocks.
  - by **country** later, when you localise (DE/NL/IT/ES/FR).
- **Rotate the sender name** between two real people (e.g. "Rene from GEL.IT.UP" / another team member) — small change, reads noticeably less like a machine.
- **Vary format** within the fixed theme: text-only "personal note" style for Compliance Corner (highest open rates), designed template with product shots for Spotlight, checklist layout for Planner.

## Rules

- Opted-in contacts only. Never import the cold lead CSV into Campaigns.
- Suppress anyone who unsubscribes everywhere (also stop 1:1 follow-ups).
- Watch two numbers: open rate (subject health) and reply/click rate (content health). If a theme underperforms twice, swap it out of the wheel.
- No Greek or Bulgarian localisation — excluded markets.
